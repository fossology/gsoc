# Community Bonding Meetings and Activities

## Community Meeting 1: Introductory Session

**Date:** May 08, 2026

### Attendees
Entire FOSSology Community, including:
- Organization Administrators
- Project Mentors
- GSoC 2026 Contributors

### Discussion
- The first community bonding session for GSoC 2026 was held.
- Organization admins, mentors, and contributors introduced themselves and shared a little about their backgrounds and open source experience.
- The FOSSology team gave an overview of the community, project goals, contribution process, and expectations for the GSoC period.
- The meeting ended with a discussion on communication and collaboration practices, followed by a short Q&A session.

### Activities Done
- Explored LicenseDB’s main features, user flows, and overall workflows from the perspective of a first-time user.
- Reviewed and understood the current information architecture and navigation patterns of the tool.
- Performed a heuristic evaluation of important screens and documented findings to share with mentors for feedback.
- Studied the existing FOSSology design system, including layouts, spacing, typography, and color guidelines to maintain consistency in upcoming designs.
